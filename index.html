Darty Time — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

Darty Time is a local darts game for Android, Android TV and Google TV, developed by Darius Hayati ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

In short: Darty Time does not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or third-party data-collecting SDKs. What you enter stays on your device, or — when you use the second-screen feature — on your own local network.

Information stored on your device

Darty Time stores the following in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Player names you type
  • Game rules and app settings
  • Match history and results
  • The Autodarts board manager address and second-screen preferences

This information is never uploaded to us or to any third party. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Network features

  • Autodarts. If you enable Autodarts, the app sends requests to the board manager address you configure, normally a device on your local network. That software is not operated by us, and the data exchanged is limited to game and throw data between your device and your board manager.
  • Second screen. When a game is shared, the hosting device runs a local HTTP server on your Wi-Fi network. A joining device sends its Android device model name and receives the live game state. The host shows the joining device's name so you can approve it. Game state and device names stay on the local network and are held only in memory for the duration of the sharing session. The read-only TV guest receives game state but does not control the game.

Darty Time does not contact any server operated by us. We do not have servers that receive your data.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state — to reach your Autodarts board manager and other devices on your local network.
  • Change Wi-Fi multicast state — to discover shared games on your local network.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.

Data retention and deletion

Local data (players, settings, matches) remains on your device until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Second-screen session data is discarded when sharing stops.

Children's privacy

Darty Time is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. The app collects no personal information, so none is transmitted to us regardless of who uses it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
